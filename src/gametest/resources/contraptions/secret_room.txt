# The secret room behind a bookshelf wall (SecretRoomGameTests, examples station 16).
# Check with: python3 tools/redstone_check.py <this file>
#
# A corridor (z 6..9) runs along a wall of bookshelves three blocks thick (z = 5
# face, z = 4 inner with a pocket at x = 5, z = 3 back); the room is behind it
# (z 0..2). The door is two bookshelves in the face (x = 4, y 1..2), like all the
# others. One bookshelf on each side of the wall at x = 8, y = 2 is the switch: a
# hidden button hangs on each side of one hidden phase block between them, so a
# click on either bookshelf presses it. Nothing redstone is visible.
#
# Signals (@name, @out<in):
#   B   a button press (either side)
#   T   the door state: a copper bulb toggles on each press, a comparator reads it
#   S1  stage 1, push the door back to z = 4: T, held by S2 delayed
#   S2  stage 2, T delayed: slide the door sideways into the pocket at x = 5
size 11 11 14
door minecraft:bookshelf
click 8 2 5 south
click 8 2 3 north
fill visible 0 0 0 8 0 9 minecraft:stone
fill visible 0 1 3 8 3 5 minecraft:oak_planks
fill visible 0 1 5 8 3 5 minecraft:bookshelf
fill visible 0 1 3 8 3 3 minecraft:bookshelf
fill visible 4 1 3 4 2 4 minecraft:air
fill visible 5 1 4 5 2 4 minecraft:air
# The pistons, before the wires that rest on them (nothing is powered while the door is closed).
# Stage 1 in the corridor pushes the door back; stage 2 slides it into the pocket.
hidden 4 1 6 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=north,extended=false] @S1
hidden 4 2 6 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=north,extended=false] @S1
hidden 3 1 4 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=east,extended=false] @S2
hidden 3 2 4 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=east,extended=false] @S2
# The switch: two buttons on one phase block, a copper bulb, a comparator.
hidden 8 2 4 phasecraft:phase_block
hidden 8 2 5 minecraft:stone_button[face=wall,facing=south,powered=false] @B
hidden 8 2 3 minecraft:stone_button[face=wall,facing=north,powered=false] @B
hidden 8 3 4 minecraft:redstone_wire[north=side,south=side,east=side,west=side] @B   # a cross: a lone dot would power only down
hidden 8 3 5 minecraft:waxed_copper_bulb[lit=false,powered=false] @B
hidden 7 2 5 minecraft:stone
hidden 7 3 5 minecraft:comparator[facing=east,mode=compare,powered=false] @T<B
# T, along the top of the wall.
hidden 6 2 5 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 3 5 minecraft:redstone_wire @T
hidden 6 2 6 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 3 6 minecraft:redstone_wire @T
hidden 6 2 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 3 4 minecraft:redstone_wire @T
hidden 5 2 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 5 3 4 minecraft:redstone_wire @T
# Stage 1: T at once, onto the wire over the corridor pistons.
hidden 5 2 6 minecraft:stone
hidden 5 3 6 minecraft:repeater[facing=east,delay=1,powered=false] @S1<T
hidden 3 2 6 minecraft:stone
hidden 4 3 6 minecraft:redstone_wire @S1
hidden 3 3 6 minecraft:redstone_wire @S1
# Stage 2: T delayed, onto the wire over the pistons inside the wall; it holds stage 1 on.
hidden 4 2 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 4 3 4 minecraft:repeater[facing=east,delay=4,powered=false] @S2<T
hidden 3 3 4 minecraft:redstone_wire @S2
hidden 3 2 5 minecraft:stone
hidden 3 3 5 minecraft:repeater[facing=north,delay=4,powered=false] @S1<S2
