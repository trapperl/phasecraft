# The trapdoor floor (TrapdoorFloorGameTests, examples station 17).
# Check with: python3 tools/redstone_check.py <this file>
#
# A corridor one block wide (x = 4, floor y = 5, walls at x = 3 and x = 5) runs
# along z. Step on the floor tile at z = 4 and the two tiles after it (z = 5, 6)
# drop away into a pit (y 2..4), then close again once nobody stands on z = 4.
# The trigger is a phased pressure plate: hidden, on a hidden phase block at the
# tile's spot. Nothing redstone is visible. Two stages, as in the staircase:
# up-facing sticky phase pistons under the tiles hold them up and let them drop
# one block; then sideways sticky phase pistons push them into pockets under
# the east wall (x = 5, y = 4). Closing runs the other way round.
#
# Signals (@name, @out<in):
#   P   the plate
#   D   "open": P at once, held on by S2 delayed (so stage 1 waits for stage 2 to pull back)
#   S1  stage 1, not D (torches): holds the tiles up while closed
#   S2  stage 2, P delayed: pushes the dropped tiles into the pockets
size 11 11 14
door minecraft:smooth_stone
step 4 6 4
fill visible 0 0 0 8 5 9 minecraft:stone
fill visible 4 5 0 4 5 9 minecraft:smooth_stone
fill visible 3 6 0 3 7 9 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill visible 5 6 0 5 7 9 minecraft:stone_bricks
fill visible 4 2 5 4 4 6 minecraft:air
fill visible 5 4 5 5 4 6 minecraft:air
# The trigger: a pressure plate on a phase block at the tile before the trap.
hidden 4 5 4 phasecraft:phase_block
hidden 4 6 4 minecraft:stone_pressure_plate[powered=false] @P
# The stage 2 pistons, before the wire that rests on them.
hidden 3 4 5 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=east,extended=false] @S2
hidden 3 4 6 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=east,extended=false] @S2
# From the phase block: D at once (east), S2 after a delay (west).
hidden 5 4 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 5 5 4 minecraft:repeater[facing=west,delay=1,powered=false] @D<P
hidden 3 4 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 3 5 4 minecraft:repeater[facing=east,delay=4,powered=false] @S2<P
# S2, onto the wire over the stage 2 pistons.
hidden 2 4 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 2 5 4 minecraft:redstone_wire @S2
hidden 2 4 5 minecraft:stone
hidden 2 5 5 minecraft:redstone_wire @S2
hidden 3 5 5 minecraft:redstone_wire @S2
hidden 3 5 6 minecraft:redstone_wire @S2
# The hold: S2 delayed into D, round the far end of the trap (z = 8).
hidden 2 4 6 minecraft:stone
hidden 2 5 6 minecraft:repeater[facing=north,delay=4,powered=false] @D<S2
hidden 2 4 7 minecraft:stone
hidden 2 5 7 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 2 4 8 minecraft:stone
hidden 2 5 8 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 3 4 8 minecraft:stone
hidden 3 5 8 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 4 4 8 minecraft:stone
hidden 4 5 8 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 5 4 8 minecraft:stone
hidden 5 5 8 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 6 4 8 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 5 8 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 6 4 7 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 5 7 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
# D down the east side, onto the blocks that hold the torches.
hidden 6 4 4 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 5 4 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 6 3 5 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 4 5 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
hidden 6 3 6 minecraft:stone
hidden 6 4 6 minecraft:redstone_wire @D
# Stage 1: not D. Wall torches beside the pistons, under the pockets.
hidden 5 3 5 minecraft:redstone_wall_torch[facing=west,lit=true] @S1<D
hidden 5 3 6 minecraft:redstone_wall_torch[facing=west,lit=true] @S1<D
# The stage 1 pistons last: extended while closed, so placed once their torches power them.
hidden 4 3 5 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=up,extended=true] @S1
hidden 4 3 6 phasecraft:sticky_phase_piston[facing=up,extended=true] @S1
